How long does a website take?

A small business site can often be prepared in about a week once content is available.

Can the website be changed after launch?

Yes. With GitHub connected to Netlify, you edit the files, push the changes, and Netlify automatically publishes the new version.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. A custom domain such as yourbusiness.com can be connected after deployment.
